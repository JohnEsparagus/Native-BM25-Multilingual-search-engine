    Findings

    Each query in the evaluation sets has a known right book. Books are split into passages, and each ranker's passages are turned into a list of books.

    Recall@3 is how often the right book appears in the top 3. MRR (mean reciprocal rank) rewards putting it first: 1 if it is first, 0.5 if second, 0.33 if third, 0 if it is missing.

    Names and specific terms (Queequeg, Odysseus, شيرلوك هولمز): BM25 is strongest, because the exact word is in the right book. Dense often confuses names, especially in Arabic and Chinese.

    Thematic queries that describe a book without its words: dense wins clearly in English, where BM25 has no words to match.

    Hybrid had the best or joint-best Recall@3 in all six tests, so it is the safest default.

    The sets are small (8–9 books, 15–42 queries each), so these are indications, not benchmarks.

    Times are for ranking every passage in the selected books. Dense includes turning the query into an embedding, and hybrid runs both rankers and then fuses their lists.
